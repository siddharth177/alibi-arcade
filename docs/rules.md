# Mafia: Alibi house rules and player behavior

This is the implemented variant, not a claim that every Mafia group plays identically.

| Participant | Information | Allowed action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| God | Every role and night choice; completion progress | Deal roles, move phases, resolve nights and votes |
| Murderer | Own identity | Target another living player at night; discuss and vote by day |
| Detective | Own identity and private investigation history | Check another living player each night; discuss and vote |
| Doctor | Own identity and their previous protection | Protect one living player each night; discuss and vote |
| Citizen | Own identity and public record | Make a harmless night check-in; discuss and vote |
| Eliminated player | Their own retained private information and public record | Spectate; no actions, votes, or town chat until the end |

There are exactly one murderer, one detective, and one doctor. God is a moderator and is never counted toward victory. Five other players are the minimum; five to nine plus God is recommended for balance.

God starts with a night after all role acknowledgements. All living players check in; a citizen's suspicion has no effect. Choices are resolved together. Protection prevents a matching attack. A submitted detective check is returned even if that detective dies during the same night. A quiet morning never identifies the attacked or protected player.

Default doctor rules allow self-protection and forbid consecutive protection of the same person. Both are configurable in the lobby. Investigation returns only murderer/not-murderer, rather than someone's exact Town role.

During discussion, living players can make claims and counterclaims. The suggested timer does not force the phase to end: God remains in control. The group supplies in-person conversation or a voice call; town text chat follows the phase restrictions.

Every living player can vote for another living player or skip. The highest tally wins. A unique winning player is eliminated; a winning skip or tie involving skip means no elimination. Default player ties open one runoff restricted to those candidates. Another tie means no elimination. God can instead choose no elimination on the first tie. Ballots are hidden until tallied; afterward the resolved ballots form a public record.

Town wins as soon as the murderer is eliminated. Mafia wins when its living count equals or exceeds the remaining Town count. The game checks after each elimination. Eliminated-role reveals are configurable; all roles are public at the end.

Disconnected players retain their role. God may wait or explicitly skip missing actions/ballots. Missing night actions have no effect and missing ballots do not count. Disconnecting never automatically eliminates a player.

Player conduct matters: keep role cards and screenshots private; use spoken claims instead of proof from the interface; compare reasons and voting records; stay silent after elimination. God should announce outcomes neutrally and avoid private-information hints. Quietness, nervousness, or confidence is not a reliable role test.
